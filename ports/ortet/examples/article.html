Ortet

A genet is a whole clonal colony. An ortet is the original individual it descends from. A ramet is any member.

What the word carries

The botanical vocabulary is doing real work here, not decoration. The interesting claim in it is that the colony and the individual are the same organism seen at two scales, and that one of those individuals is the one everything else was cut from. That is a claim about lineage, and lineage is exactly what a reference host is for.

A reference individual is not the best individual. It is the one you go back to when you want to know what the stock actually is, before any of the grafting. It should therefore be small enough to hold in the head, and honest about what it does not do.

Reading it the other way

The metaphor also constrains what may be added. A ramet that grew a second root system and started fixing its own nitrogen would no longer be telling you anything about the stock. Every feature that makes the reference host more pleasant to use makes it less useful as a reference, which is an unusual and clarifying pressure to design under.

So the list of things this document's host will never have is longer than the list of things it has, and the long list is the point rather than an apology for an unfinished program.

Where the boundary falls

Everything above the engine — the tabs, the tiles, the reader lane, the settings, the persistence — belongs to the product. Everything the product cannot be written without belongs to the engine. A host that sits exactly on that line, and links nothing from above it, is a witness that the line is real and not merely drawn on a diagram.

The witness is worth more than the host. The host is a few hundred lines and could be rewritten in an afternoon; the fact that its dependency cone can be enumerated and found clean is the thing that stops the line from quietly moving.

Propagation

Cuttings taken from the ortet are ramets, and ramets are genetically the ortet. Nothing in the colony is new material. What differs between them is entirely position: light, water, what they happen to be growing next to.

That is roughly the relationship between this host and every richer one. None of them contain engine code the others lack. They differ in what they are wired to, and in what they have been asked to be responsible for.

Which is why the smallest one is the one worth keeping honest. If it can open a document, lay it out, paint it, and follow a link, then so can all of them, and any failure further up is a failure of wiring rather than of the engine.
